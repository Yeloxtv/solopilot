# Kickoff workshop, 22 September 2026

Harbor & Pine head office. Present: Maya Lindqvist (operations director), two managers, one inspector, the design team, Alex (developer). Fictional notes, written for the solopilot demo.

## Decided

- **Severity of a finding**: four levels, low, medium, high and critical. Critical findings are shown first everywhere.
- **Checklist versions**: an inspection keeps the version of the checklist it was run with, even after a new version is published. Past reports must not change.
- **Photos**: resized on the server, and their location data removed before they are stored.
- **The demo of the inspection flow** to the managers comes before anything else is shown.
